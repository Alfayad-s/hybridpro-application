Hybrid Pro is a mobile-first Progressive Web App for athletes who want workouts, meals, recovery, and coaching in one place. The product promise is: know what you lifted, what you ate, and what still needs rest — then decide what comes next.

## What Hybrid Pro does

Hybrid Pro is a training companion, not a generic gym brochure. After sign-in, people use it to:

- Log workouts and follow planned training days
- Browse and customize an exercise library with muscle targeting
- Plan meals and log food
- Track muscle-group recovery so they know what still needs rest
- Review personal records and workout history
- Upload body-composition reports and get analysis
- Chat with an in-app AI coach that can suggest exercises and interpret training context
- Join challenges and see rewards
- Measure height with on-device AR on the profile screen
- See local weather on the bottom navigation to inform outdoor training

## How people start

1. Open https://app.hybridpro.in/
2. Tap Start training or Sign in
3. Create or sign into an account with email and password, email OTP, or Google
4. Land on /dashboard and use the bottom navigation for training, meals, recovery, and AI

Google sign-in is provided through Supabase Auth. The OAuth return path is https://app.hybridpro.in/auth/callback.
